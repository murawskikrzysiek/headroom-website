Sounds right, fails at delivery

A finished master still has to survive Spotify, Apple Music and the rest.

Four things go wrong between the bounce and the upload, and none of them is audible on a quiet pass through the monitors. The integrated loudness overshoots and Spotify turns the track down. A quiet master stays quiet on Apple Music, which only turns loud tracks down. The true peak clears −1 dBTP in the WAV and lands over it after the AAC encode. And a stereo image that thins out in mono loses its vocal on a phone speaker. Each of these is a number the file already contains, and the delivery card reads them out as soon as the file has loaded.

GainWhat each checked platform does to the level, in a sentence: "Spotify turns this down by 5.0 dB."
PeakThe 4× oversampled true peak against the platform's ceiling: the inter-sample peak a limiter or a decoder meets, not the sample magnitude.
CodecApple Music's AAC encode, made and measured on the Mac: its true peak and its samples over full scale.
MonoLeft against right over the whole file and over every second of it, with the stretches that fall below −0.2 correlation.
WhereThe TP threshold under the loudness graph marks every stretch of the file over a ceiling on the waveform, so one number becomes the moments to fix.

The delivery check

Deliver to Music streaming, read the card, check the encode and the mono fold-down, hear it at the platform's level, fix, export.

1 · Deliver to Music streaming
Open the master. Deliver to, over the delivery card, names what the file is judged against, such as Deliver to Spotify · Music streaming. Its picker (Analyze › Deliver To…, ⇧⌘D) lists the category's targets: up to four checked, one of them primary, the target the verdict leads with. The card judges the whole file once it has loaded, and its readings hold still while the file plays; Loudness details' Live side shows what is playing.
2 · Read the verdicts
The primary target's verdict is a sentence: a mix at −9 LUFS reads "Spotify turns this down by 5.0 dB." The other checked targets follow one line each: Apple Music −7.0 dB, YouTube −5.0 dB, Tidal −5.0 dB. A true peak over the primary's ceiling adds to the sentence: "True peak −0.4 dBTP is over its −1 dBTP ceiling." The readings take one set of colours from the primary target: green within 1.5 dB of its reference or where it plays the file as recorded, yellow up to 4 dB away, orange beyond that or for a true peak over the ceiling. Loudness details holds the integrated loudness, loudness range, max momentary and short-term, true peak, sample peak and the headroom, measured from the true peak.
3 · Check the encode and the fold-down
Under Music streaming the card checks two more things. Check Apple Music's AAC encode (Analyze › Check the Apple Music Encode) encodes the file as Apple's afconvert does, AAC at 256 kbps, constrained VBR and quality 127 (128 kbps for mono), decodes it and measures it: "After Apple Music's AAC encode the true peak is +0.4 dBTP, with 12 samples over full scale." The mono line reads left against right over the whole file and every second of it: "Mono-compatible. Correlation +0.62 over the file.", or how long and from where the correlation stays below −0.2, with Select the longest stretch to play that part.
4 · Hear it at the platform's level
Hear at Spotify's level plays the master with Spotify's gain, through a true-peak limiter at Spotify's ceiling. It reads Stop hearing at Spotify's level while it is on, and Playback › Hear at Spotify's Level does the same. The other targets offer it in their Fix menu. It appears only where the platform changes the level, and the file itself is not changed.
5 · Fix
Limit peaks to −1.0 dBTP opens the Edit task (⌘2) with Limit True Peak set to the target's ceiling; after a codec check, the card's own Limit peaks sets the ceiling less what the encode added. Normalize to −14 LUFS opens Normalize with the target filled in: one gain for the whole file, with a true-peak limiter holding the ceiling when the gain raises the level. A fix appears only where it changes something. Both panels also work on a selection, so one hot passage can be limited on its own. Every edit joins the Edit task's History by name, up to 16 steps back, and the file is measured again in the background, so the card shows the edited master. More in the guide.
6 · Compare with the encode
Hear the encode in Compare (Analyze › Compare with the Apple Music Encode) puts the encode in a slot beside the master. Compare (⌘3) shows both in its matrix with their differences, and the encode's residual, D, plays what the encoder took away.
7 · Export the report
File › Export… (⌘E) writes the PDF, HTML and JSON reports into one folder, named after the file; ⇧⌘E and ⌥⌘E open the sheet with the JSON or the PDF ticked. The reports name the category and its targets, "Judged against: Spotify (primary), Apple Music, YouTube, Tidal", carry every checked target's verdict and, with the Codec check switch on, as it starts under Music streaming, the master beside its encode.

Not every platform boosts

Apple Music's Sound Check, YouTube and Tidal only turn loud tracks down. A master at −18 LUFS plays at −18 on Apple Music, not at its −16 reference, and the card says so: "Apple Music plays this as recorded, 2.0 dB under its −16 LUFS reference." It reads green and offers no level fix.

Spotify (in its default mode), Amazon Music, Deezer and SoundCloud raise quiet tracks, so their "turns this up by" is a real gain, and Hear at their level plays it.

Music streaming holds Spotify (−14 LUFS), Apple Music (−16), YouTube (−14), Tidal (−14), Amazon Music (−14, with a −2 dBTP ceiling), Deezer (−15), SoundCloud (−14) and the vendor-neutral AES TD1008 (−18). Every target's reference, tolerance and ceiling is in the user guide, and a custom target joins the category beside them.

Section by section

On macOS 27, Apple's on-device music analysis reads a song's sections, bars and stems, and Specula measures against them with its own BS.1770-4: each section's loudness on the timeline, shaded from quieter to louder, the vocal's loudness against the bed, and the sections as chapters for a table of per-section readings.
