Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Lyra is a local macOS utility. The app itself does not collect, transmit, or store personal data. There are no in-app analytics and no crash reporting. The app does reach the network for license validation, auto-update checks (which are also counted, without personal data), the URL handoff to Audita, and feedback you choose to send; each is described below.

Data collected

No personal data, unless you leave an email address with feedback (see Feedback form). Settings and reference levels stay on your Mac. Update checks are counted anonymously, as described under Auto-update checks.

Accessibility permission

Lyra requests Accessibility access in System Settings. This permission is used solely to intercept the F10, F11, and F12 key presses system-wide. No other input is read or recorded.

UA Mixer Engine communication

The app communicates with UA Mixer Engine through a local TCP connection on your machine. This communication stays entirely on your device - no data leaves your computer.

License validation

When you activate or validate a license key, the app contacts the Lemon Squeezy licensing API to verify your key. Only your license key and a machine name are sent. No personal data is transmitted beyond what is required for activation. If the app cannot reach the server (e.g. you're offline), it grants the benefit of the doubt and continues to run normally.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, an external payment provider. When you purchase the app, Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to the transaction. Headroom does not receive or store your payment details. You can review Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

Audita pairing

When you use Audita's "Send to Lyra…" feature, Audita opens a lyra:// URL on the same Mac to hand a slot number, a name (e.g. "K-14"), and a dB SPL value to Lyra. The handoff happens entirely between two local apps via macOS's URL handler. Nothing leaves your device.

Website analytics

The headroomstudio.dev website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-first analytics service. It records aggregate pageviews, top referrers, and country (no individual user tracking, no cookies, no GDPR cookie banner required). This applies to the website only; the Lyra app does not load these scripts.

Newsletter signup

If you sign up for the Headroom newsletter via the form on the website, your email address is sent to MailerLite, our newsletter provider. MailerLite's privacy policy applies. We use it only to send occasional release announcements; you can unsubscribe from any email. The newsletter form lives on the website and does not run inside the app.

Auto-update checks

Lyra uses Sparkle for automatic updates. About once a day it fetches the appcast at https://headroomstudio.dev/lyra/appcast.xml to check for new versions. The request carries the Lyra and Sparkle version numbers and one word for the license state: trial, licensed or expired. No license key, email address, machine name or device identifier is sent.

These requests are counted to see how many copies of Lyra are in use, by version, license state and country. Each copy is counted through a hash of its IP address and the app's name, salted with a key that changes every month, so one Mac cannot be followed from month to month. The IP address itself is never stored.

Feedback form

Lyra shows a short feedback form twice during the trial: a few days in, and when the trial ends. The same form opens from the menu at any time. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

A submission carries the options you pick and the text you type, plus the Lyra version, the macOS version, which of the three prompts it came from, the license state (trial, licensed or expired) and, during a trial, the trial day. No license key, machine name or device identifier is sent. The IP address is used only for a rate limit on sending and is never stored. Feedback without an email address is anonymous.

The email address field is optional. The address is kept only if you also allow a reply, and is used only to reply to that message. It is deleted 90 days after the reply, or 90 days after you send the feedback if there is no reply. The rest of the submission is deleted 12 months after you send it.

Submissions go to a Cloudflare Worker run by Headroom Studio and are stored in a Cloudflare database.

Your data rights

If you're an EU or UK resident, you have rights under GDPR to access, rectify, port, or delete personal data we hold about you. In practice, Lyra holds no personal data on its own. The only personal data linked to you anywhere is what Lemon Squeezy receives at checkout (email address, billing info), what MailerLite holds (email address) if you've subscribed, and your email address if you left one with feedback. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@headroomstudio.dev and we'll route the request to the relevant sub-processor.

Uninstalling Lyra

To remove Lyra and its local data:

  1. Quit Lyra from the menu bar.
  2. Drag Lyra from /Applications to the Trash.
  3. To also clear settings (saved reference levels, HUD preference, target device): defaults delete com.headroom.Lyra in Terminal.

Your license key survives this and reactivates on a fresh install. To clear the license too, deactivate it first via Menu → Manage License → Deactivate before uninstalling.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at hello@headroomstudio.dev.
