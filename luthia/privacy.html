Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Luthia is a guitar practice app for Mac, iPad and iPhone. It has no user accounts and no telemetry of any kind (no analytics, no crash reports), and it never contacts a Headroom Studio server. Everything it stores lives on your devices and, if you keep sync on, in your own iCloud account. The two network paths it uses belong to Apple and are described below.

Microphone and audio input

Luthia asks for microphone permission so it can hear the guitar: timing against the click in Train, chord and note recognition in Chords and Fretboard, and the tuner. Audio is analysed in real time on the device to produce onset times, pitches and chord names. It is never transmitted.

Train keeps the raw audio of the most recent sessions on the device, in the app's own storage, so a take can be looked at again; the eight most recent are kept and older ones are removed. These recordings never leave the device and are not part of iCloud sync. Deleting the app deletes them.

What Luthia stores

Your library: the exercises you compose, generate or import, custom progressions, the practice plan and log, fretboard progress and Train history. Each record is a small file in the app's own storage.

Settings that stay on each device: the input device, the sound, tuner and notation settings, the drill setup and a random device tag that lets the app tell its own practice-log entries apart from another device's.

Mac: ~/Library/Containers/com.headroom.Luthia/ · iPhone and iPad: the app's sandbox

iCloud sync

With Settings › iCloud › Sync with iCloud on (the default when a device is signed in), the library above is stored in an iCloud container that belongs to your Apple Account, and iCloud Drive keeps it in step across your devices. The data goes from your device to Apple's servers and back to your other devices; Headroom Studio has no access to it and no server of its own. Apple's privacy policy covers the transport and storage.

Turning sync off leaves a full local copy on the device. Signing out of iCloud does the same. Deleting the data from every device removes it from the container as well.

Files you import

Import reads the Guitar Pro, MusicXML, MuseScore, MIDI, Luthia and text files you choose through the system file picker, a drop, or Open With. A file is read in place and never written back; what Luthia keeps is its own copy of the exercise in the library. Export writes only where you pick in the save panel or the Files sheet.

In-app purchase

Luthia Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by the App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the purchase record; Luthia asks the App Store whether the purchase exists on the signed-in account and reads nothing else. Headroom Studio receives no personal data from the transaction and no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Updates

Luthia is distributed through the App Store, and updates arrive the same way. The app itself never checks for updates and never downloads anything.
